    About this project

    Business question. Where is revenue growing, where is it at risk, and where should delivery leaders hire and allocate people next?

    Approach. Monthly billing data (person-months × rate) by client geography, service line and engagement, compared year on year using the Indian financial year (April to March). Anomalies are months that move more than 30% away from an engagement group’s usual year-over-year trend.

    Tools. PostgreSQL for the queries, Python and pandas for the data and analysis scripts, plain JavaScript for this dashboard.
